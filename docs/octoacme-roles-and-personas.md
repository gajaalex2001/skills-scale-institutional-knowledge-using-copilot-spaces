# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, organizational support, and authority for decisions that extend beyond the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Define the strategic intent, expected outcomes, and success measures
- Secure funding, resources, and organizational support
- Approve major scope or priority changes when escalation is required
- Resolve high-impact issues and remove enterprise-level barriers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on business outcomes and with the Project Manager on governance, major risks, and delivery constraints.
- Receive concise status and decision requests from the Project Manager.
- Support the Product Manager and delivery team when cross-organizational alignment or investment is needed.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify needs, processes, and constraints so that solutions have well-defined, traceable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate discovery and elicit requirements from stakeholders
- Document current and future processes, assumptions, and dependencies
- Maintain traceability between business needs, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Identify gaps, conflicting needs, and process improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to refine problem statements and product requirements.
- Work with the Project Manager to surface requirements-related risks and dependencies.
- Help Developers and quality roles translate requirements into feasible designs and testable criteria.

---

## Technical Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architecture and implementation decisions while balancing technical quality, delivery constraints, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and establish relevant design standards
- Review architectural options, dependencies, and significant technical risks
- Guide technical planning and ensure solutions are implementable and maintainable
- Coach Developers on design, code quality, and operational concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with the Product Manager on feasibility, trade-offs, and sequencing.
- Work with the Project Manager to identify constraints, dependencies, and delivery risks.
- Mentor and coordinate Developers while coordinating quality, security, and infrastructure concerns with appropriate specialists.

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and coordinate the strategy used to verify that deliverables meet functional, non-functional, and release expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and required quality gates
- Coordinate test planning, automation, coverage, and release readiness
- Track defects, quality risks, and unresolved readiness gaps
- Recommend entry and exit criteria for testing and release stages

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to clarify defects, testability, and remediation priorities.
- Work with the Product Manager to align acceptance criteria and quality expectations.
- Advise the Project Manager on quality risk, testing effort, and release dependencies.

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads identify applicable obligations and help the project design controls that address security, privacy, regulatory, and policy risk.

### Responsibilities
- Establish applicable security and compliance requirements
- Review risk assessments, control designs, exceptions, and evidence
- Advise teams on secure implementation and policy requirements
- Escalate unresolved high-risk findings through established governance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with the Technical Lead on architecture and control implementation.
- Work with the Product Manager and Project Manager to incorporate obligations, trade-offs, and decision timing.
- Support Developers and QA in implementing and verifying required controls.

---

## User Experience / Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Leads represent user needs and ensure that interactions are usable, accessible, coherent, and consistent with product intent.

### Responsibilities
- Lead discovery, interaction design, and usability validation
- Maintain relevant design patterns and accessibility standards
- Translate user and product needs into designs and prototypes
- Validate implemented experiences against design intent

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with the Product Manager on user problems, outcomes, and prioritization.
- Coordinate with Developers and the Technical Lead on implementation constraints and feasibility.
- Work with the Project Manager to plan research, design, and validation activities.

---

## Stakeholder / Customer Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder and Customer Representatives provide domain knowledge and a direct view of the needs, behaviors, and outcomes of people affected by the project.

### Responsibilities
- Supply business, operational, or customer context
- Review proposed solutions and provide timely feedback
- Participate in validation of requirements and delivered outcomes
- Communicate relevant concerns to and from the wider stakeholder group

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Support the Product Manager by validating needs, priorities, usability, and outcomes.
- Provide the Project Manager with domain context and stakeholder input.
- Collaborate with Business Analysts and delivery teams to clarify requirements and acceptance evidence.

---

## Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Representatives help the project prepare for production, ongoing support, monitoring, and operational ownership.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational support, monitoring, maintenance, and capacity needs
- Contribute runbooks, support models, escalation paths, and training needs
- Identify rollout risks that affect users or production operations
- Represent operational readiness during planning and release decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with the Technical Lead and Developers on monitoring, deployment, and observability needs.
- Work with the Project Manager and Quality Assurance Lead on readiness and release dependencies.
- Partner with the Product Manager and Support stakeholders on expected usage, support content, and rollout plans.
